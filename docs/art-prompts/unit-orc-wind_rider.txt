============================================================

A complete 16-frame character sprite sheet of "Wind Rider" for a tower defense game, HD pixel art.

STYLE: HD pixel art in the 16-bit era JRPG / Warcraft III style - crisp pixel clusters, one clean 1px dark outline around the whole silhouette, a strictly limited palette of about 12-16 colours, soft dithered shading and a subtle cool rim light from the upper left. NOT vector, NOT smooth digital painting, NOT a 3D render, NOT chibi-cute, NOT anime lineart. Dark fantasy mood, muted saturation, strong value contrast so the shape stays readable against dark brown dirt terrain.

SUBJECT: an orc wind rider on a wyvern. FLYER, not a ground walker, faces RIGHT. A lean leathery wyvern in dark leather brown and olive green with broad bat-like wings, a long neck, a narrow fanged head and a long barbed tail curling behind; on its back sits a small hunched orc rider in rust-orange cloth and blackened iron shoulder plates, holding a short spear and a knotted rein. The pair must LOOK fast and reckless: wings swept back in long strokes, the rider leaning forward, the tail streaming.

PALETTE (orc faction, use these and nothing else, plus the per-subject additions listed below): rust orange #d97b39, dark leather brown, blackened iron grey, worn steel grey, red warpaint, bone white, olive-green orc skin, near-black outline. Per-subject additions: none.

CONSISTENCY - THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE: the SAME creature appears in all 16 cells. Identical proportions, identical colours, identical gear and markings, identical scale and identical eye level in every cell. Only the pose and the camera direction change between cells.

SHEET LAYOUT: one square image, a grid of 4 columns x 4 rows = 16 equal square cells, read left to right, top row first. Each cell is one pixel-art frame rendered for a final size of 32x32 px, so keep the detail budget low enough to survive that downscale.

WITHIN EACH ROW the four poses must be visibly DIFFERENT from each other - never draw the same pose twice, exaggerate the motion if needed.

SILHOUETTE at 28x28 px: a wide-winged beast with a long thin tail trailing behind and a small rider lump on its back - the only orc shape with wings, never confused with the ground walkers.

BACKGROUND: one flat solid magenta #FF00FF across the whole image, behind and between the cells. No transparency, no gradient, no texture, no ground line, no horizontal stroke, no shadow under the subject, no glow bleeding onto the magenta.

FRAMING: each subject centred in its own cell with about 10% empty margin on every side, the body at a constant hover height inside each row, nothing cropped. No borders, frames or grid lines drawn between cells.

FORBIDDEN: no text, numbers, labels or watermarks anywhere in the image; no extra props, scenery or effects; never two subjects in one cell; do not change the camera angle inside a row.
