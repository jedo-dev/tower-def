============================================================

A complete 16-frame character sprite sheet of "Wisp" for a tower defense game, HD pixel art.

STYLE: HD pixel art in the 16-bit era JRPG / Warcraft III style - crisp pixel clusters, one clean 1px dark outline around the whole silhouette, a strictly limited palette of about 12-16 colours, soft dithered shading and a subtle cool rim light from the upper left. NOT vector, NOT smooth digital painting, NOT a 3D render, NOT chibi-cute, NOT anime lineart. Dark fantasy mood, muted saturation, strong value contrast so the shape stays readable against a dark desaturated forest floor.

SUBJECT: an elven wisp. A DRIFTING SPIRIT, no body, no limbs, no face. A small bright core of moonlit green light the size of a fist, wrapped in a halo of paler teal glow, with three or four thin trailing threads of light streaming behind it like a comet. No armour, no weapon, no features. It must LOOK weightless and quick: a darting hover, the trail the only thing that tells which way it is going.

PALETTE (elf faction, use only these, plus the per-subject additions listed below): moonlit green #6bbf89, silver, pale birch wood, teal glow accents, deep forest shadow as the darkest value. Per-subject additions: a bright pale teal-white used ONLY for the core.

CONSISTENCY - THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE: the SAME creature appears in all 16 cells. Identical proportions, identical colours, identical gear and markings, identical scale and identical eye level in every cell. Only the pose and the camera direction change between cells.

SHEET LAYOUT: one square image, a grid of 4 columns x 4 rows = 16 equal square cells, read left to right, top row first. Each cell is one pixel-art frame rendered for a final size of 32x32 px, so keep the detail budget low enough to survive that downscale.

ROW 1 (cells 1-4) - MOVING TOWARD THE VIEWER, front view: the core drifting at the viewer, the trailing wisps streaming back and away.
  cell 1 - the core small and tight, the trailing wisps gathered close behind it.
  cell 2 - the core swelling brighter, the trailing wisps flaring out wide to the left and right.
  cell 3 - the core at its largest, the wisps streaming straight back in long thin threads.
  cell 4 - the core shrinking again, the wisps curling inward, leading cleanly into the first frame.

ROW 2 (cells 5-8) - MOVING SIDEWAYS, strict profile, facing RIGHT: the core leading, the wisps trailing to the LEFT.
  cell 5 - the core small and tight, the trail short and gathered.
  cell 6 - the core brighter, the trail flaring up and down in two curling threads.
  cell 7 - the core at its largest, the trail at its longest, straight back.
  cell 8 - the core shrinking, the trail curling inward, leading into the first frame.

ROW 3 (cells 9-12) - MOVING AWAY FROM THE VIEWER, back view: the trailing wisps toward the viewer, the core beyond them.
  cell 9 - the core small, the wisps gathered close in front of it.
  cell 10 - the core brighter, the wisps flaring out wide.
  cell 11 - the core at its largest, the wisps streaming toward the viewer in long thin threads.
  cell 12 - the core shrinking, the wisps curling inward, leading into the first frame.

ROW 4 (cells 13-16) - DEATH, side view facing RIGHT, same camera as row 2.
  cell 13 STRUCK - the core splitting into a bright ring, the trail snapping outward.
  cell 14 BURSTING - the ring expanding, the core gone, a scatter of bright motes.
  cell 15 SCATTERED - a loose cloud of dim motes drifting apart, no core left.
  cell 16 FADING - a few last faint motes, mostly gone.

WITHIN EACH ROW the four poses must be visibly DIFFERENT from each other - never draw the same pose twice, exaggerate the motion if needed.

SILHOUETTE at 28x28 px: a single small bright dot with thin comet threads trailing from it - by far the smallest and simplest shape in the roster, mostly empty cell around it.

BACKGROUND: one flat solid magenta #FF00FF across the whole image, behind and between the cells. No transparency, no gradient, no texture, no ground line, no horizontal stroke, no shadow under the subject, no glow bleeding onto the magenta.

FRAMING: each subject centred in its own cell with about 10% empty margin on every side, the body at a constant hover height inside each row, nothing cropped. No borders, frames or grid lines drawn between cells.

FORBIDDEN: no text, numbers, labels or watermarks anywhere in the image; no extra props, scenery or effects; never two subjects in one cell; do not change the camera angle inside a row.
