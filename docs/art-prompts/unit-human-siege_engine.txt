A complete 16-frame character sprite sheet of "Siege Engine" for a tower defense game, HD pixel art.

STYLE: 16-bit era JRPG / Warcraft III style pixel art. Crisp pixel clusters, one 1px dark outline around the whole
silhouette, a limited palette of about 12-16 colours, soft dithered shading and a subtle cool rim light from the upper
left. Dark fantasy mood, muted saturation, strong value contrast so the machine stays readable against pale grey stone
terrain. NOT vector art, NOT smooth digital painting, NOT a 3D render, NOT chibi-cute.

SUBJECT: a plated war machine that crawls forward and refuses to die. A low armoured wagon body of riveted iron plates
over a heavy timber frame, two large iron-shod spoked wheels, a reinforced sloped ram plate at the front, a small
bolted crew hatch with a narrow viewing slit on the upper hull. NO visible legs, NO visible draught animal, NO rider on
top — it moves by itself on its wheels.

PALETTE (human faction): steel blue #5e94d6, polished steel, gold trim, grey stone, deep red cloth. Mostly grey riveted
steel plating and dark oiled iron, with a steel blue painted panel on the flank, a thin gold trim line along the top
edge and a small deep red pennant or cloth strip on the hull.

CONSISTENCY - THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE: the SAME creature appears in all 16 cells. Identical proportions, identical colours, identical gear and markings, identical scale and identical eye level in every cell. Only the pose and the camera direction change between cells.

SHEET LAYOUT: one square image, a grid of 4 columns x 4 rows = 16 equal square cells, read left to right, top row first. Each cell is one pixel-art frame rendered for a final size of 32x32 px, so keep the detail budget low enough to survive that downscale.

SILHOUETTE READ AT 28x28 PX: a low, wide, heavy rectangle sitting on two big circles — a squat brick on wheels, clearly

BACKGROUND: one flat solid magenta #FF00FF across the whole image, behind and between the cells. No transparency, no gradient, no texture, no ground line, no horizontal stroke, no shadow under the subject, no glow bleeding onto the magenta.

FRAMING: each subject centred in its own cell with about 10% empty margin on every side, feet on a consistent baseline inside each row, nothing cropped. No borders, frames or grid lines drawn between cells.

FORBIDDEN: no text, numbers, labels or watermarks anywhere in the image; no extra props, scenery or effects; never two subjects in one cell; do not change the camera angle inside a row.
