A complete 16-frame character sprite sheet of "Gryphon Rider" for a tower defense game, HD pixel art.

STYLE: HD pixel art in the 16-bit era JRPG / Warcraft III style - crisp pixel clusters, one clean 1px dark outline around the whole silhouette, a strictly limited palette of about 12-16 colours, soft dithered shading and a subtle cool rim light from the upper left. NOT vector, NOT smooth digital painting, NOT a 3D render, NOT chibi-cute, NOT anime lineart. Dark fantasy mood, muted saturation, strong value contrast so the shape stays readable against pale grey stone terrain.

SUBJECT: a dwarven gryphon rider. FLYER, not a ground walker, faces RIGHT. A huge gryphon with the forequarters, beak and broad feathered wings of an eagle and the hindquarters and tail of a lion, its chest plated in polished steel with gold trim and a steel blue saddle cloth; on its back a stocky bearded dwarf in steel plate with a deep red cloak, holding a heavy storm hammer overhead. The pair must LOOK massive and armoured: wide slow wing strokes, the hammer the brightest point, the lion tail trailing.

PALETTE (human faction, use these and nothing else, plus the per-subject additions listed below): steel blue #5e94d6, polished steel, gold trim, grey stone, deep red cloth, near-black outline. Per-subject additions: none.

CONSISTENCY - THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE: the SAME creature appears in all 16 cells. Identical proportions, identical colours, identical gear and markings, identical scale and identical eye level in every cell. Only the pose and the camera direction change between cells.
