============================================================

A complete 16-frame character sprite sheet of "Druid of the Claw" for a tower defense game, HD pixel art.

STYLE: HD pixel art in the 16-bit era JRPG / Warcraft III style - crisp pixel clusters, one clean 1px dark outline around the whole silhouette, a strictly limited palette of about 12-16 colours, soft dithered shading and a subtle cool rim light from the upper left. NOT vector, NOT smooth digital painting, NOT a 3D render, NOT chibi-cute, NOT anime lineart. Dark fantasy mood, muted saturation, strong value contrast so the shape stays readable against a dark desaturated forest floor.

SUBJECT: an elven druid in bear form, a GROUND WALKER on four paws (no wings, no flight). A huge shaggy bear with fur in moonlit green shading to deep forest shadow, pale birch-wood antlers growing from its brow, silver runes glowing faintly along its shoulders and moss and small leaves caught in the fur of its back. Long claws, a heavy low head, a short tail. It must LOOK massive and slow: a rolling four-legged lumber with the shoulders rising above the head.

PALETTE (elf faction, use only these, plus the per-subject additions listed below): moonlit green #6bbf89, silver, pale birch wood, teal glow accents, deep forest shadow as the darkest value. Per-subject additions: none.

CONSISTENCY - THE MOST IMPORTANT RULE: the SAME creature appears in all 16 cells. Identical proportions, identical colours, identical gear and markings, identical scale and identical eye level in every cell. Only the pose and the camera direction change between cells.

SHEET LAYOUT: one square image, a grid of 4 columns x 4 rows = 16 equal square cells, read left to right, top row first. Each cell is one pixel-art frame rendered for a final size of 32x32 px, so keep the detail budget low enough to survive that downscale.

ROW 1 (cells 1-4) - MOVING TOWARD THE VIEWER, front view: head, chest and the front legs visible, the rider or the back of the beast rising behind.
  cell 1 CONTACT - the same phase seen from the front: front legs spread wide in a long stride, the body low.
  cell 2 GATHER - the same phase seen from the front: legs drawn together under the body, the head lifted.
  cell 3 CONTACT MIRRORED - the same phase seen from the front: the opposite legs leading, wide stride, the body low again.
  cell 4 SUSPENSION - the same phase seen from the front: legs tucked, the body at its HIGHEST point of the stride.

ROW 2 (cells 5-8) - MOVING SIDEWAYS, strict profile, facing RIGHT.
  cell 5 CONTACT - front legs reaching far forward, hind legs stretched back, the longest silhouette of the cycle, body low.
  cell 6 GATHER - all four legs drawn in under the body, back arched, head lifted.
  cell 7 CONTACT MIRRORED - the opposite diagonal of legs leading, the stride wide again, body low.
  cell 8 SUSPENSION - legs tucked off the ground, body at its HIGHEST point, back flat.

ROW 3 (cells 9-12) - MOVING AWAY FROM THE VIEWER, back view: the hindquarters and the back of the head visible, no face.
  cell 9 CONTACT - the same phase seen from the back: hind legs spread in a long stride, the body low.
  cell 10 GATHER - the same phase seen from the back: legs drawn together, the head lifted.
  cell 11 CONTACT MIRRORED - the same phase seen from the back: the opposite legs leading, the body low again.
  cell 12 SUSPENSION - the same phase seen from the back: legs tucked, the body at its HIGHEST point of the stride.

ROW 4 (cells 13-16) - DEATH, side view facing RIGHT, same camera as row 2.
  cell 13 IMPACT - struck: head thrown back, front legs buckling, the body twisting away from the blow.
  cell 14 FALLING - collapsing sideways, legs folding, the body dropping toward the ground.
  cell 15 DOWN - lying on its side, legs sprawled, silhouette low and flat.
  cell 16 FADING - the body mostly gone, only a faint flattened dark shape and a few scattered pixels of dust.

WITHIN EACH ROW the four poses must be visibly DIFFERENT from each other - never draw the same pose twice, exaggerate the motion if needed.

SILHOUETTE at 28x28 px: a wide, low, heavy four-legged block with a hump of shoulders and the antler fork rising from the head - the broadest ground shape in the elven roster, never confused with the mounted huntress.

BACKGROUND: one flat solid magenta #FF00FF across the whole image, behind and between the cells. No transparency, no gradient, no texture, no ground line, no horizontal stroke, no shadow under the subject, no glow bleeding onto the magenta.

FRAMING: each subject centred in its own cell with about 10% empty margin on every side, feet on a consistent baseline inside each row, nothing cropped. No borders, frames or grid lines drawn between cells.

FORBIDDEN: no text, numbers, labels or watermarks anywhere in the image; no extra props, scenery or effects; never two subjects in one cell; do not change the camera angle inside a row.
